Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy. Chlorophyll in the chloroplasts absorbs sunlight, mainly in the red and blue wavelengths. My own view is that this is one of the most important reactions in nature. Plants also lose water through small pores called stomata, which they open and close depending on the weather. In the Calvin cycle, carbon dioxide from the air is fixed into glucose using that stored energy. Farmers can improve crop yields by managing irrigation, soil nutrients and planting density carefully.
